## Workflow
1. **Gather list** — start from opportunities with evidence.
2. **Define criteria** — explicit criteria (e.g. impact, frequency, reach, effort) with definitions.
3. **Score consistently** — apply same rubric to all items.
4. **Document trade-offs** — note assumptions and trade-offs.
5. **Rank with rationale** — produce ranked list with why each ranks as it does.
6. **Output** — prioritized scope ready for handoff.

Do not rely on gut feel alone; make reasoning explicit.
