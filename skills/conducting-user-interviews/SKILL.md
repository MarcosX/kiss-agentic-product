## Guardrails
- Open-ended before options. Start with "walk me through the last time…" and only offer choices once the story is exhausted.
- Never label an option "Recommended" while gathering evidence; recommendations belong to definition.
- Do not name features, deliverables, or roadmap slices in discovery questions.
- Capture at least one verbatim quote per round in `interview-notes.md` before asking the next round.
- Record what surprised you, not only what confirmed the hypothesis.

## Workflow
1. **Plan** — define goal, target user, hypotheses to test (not assume).
2. **Prepare questions** — open-ended, context-focused, avoid leading questions.
3. **Conduct** — listen actively, ask follow-ups, capture quotes/context.
4. **Capture evidence** — write `interview-notes.md` with quotes, observations, and round summaries.
5. **Synthesize** — extract themes with supporting evidence.
6. **Output** — interview insights ready for opportunity discovery.

Focus on understanding, not pitching.
